# Occupancy and ADR

After monitoringRevPAR,use the Occupancy and ADR chart for a closer look at Average Daily Rate (ADR) in relationship to occupancy. For example, the RevPAR for one day might be much better than last year, and you might want to know in which of the two metrics the change occurred.
Click to see anoverview of all panels of the dashboard.

## Chart Steps

### Accessing the Chart

- Clickand thenAt a Glance.
- Scroll down the page to the Occupancy and ADR chart.

### Interacting with the Chart

- To view Occupancy % only, 
	 click the label for each year in the legend to hide ADR data.
- To view occupancy data, point to a desired date to 
	 view the percentage. Past dates show actual ADR and occupancy values for both this year and last year. Current or future dates show forecasted values for this year compared to the actual values for last year.
- To view ADR only, click 
	 each Occupancy % label in the legend to hide occupancy data. Point to the desired data points to see 
	 the data for that day.
