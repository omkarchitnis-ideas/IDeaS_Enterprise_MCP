# Group Products

Use group products to provide fast pricing and availability for groups, withoutcalculating their profit and displacement like aGroup Pricing evaluationdoes. Similar toLinked Products,G3 RMSoptimizes the pricing for group products that are linked to BAR. Examples include inquiries for small groups and immediate quotes for your brand website, call center, or third-party booking channel.
Review theBest Practicesto learn more.

## Steps to Set Up Group Products

On the Pricing Configuration page, you can view, edit, and add group products. Follow these steps to set up a new product.
- Click, thenDecisions, and thenPricing.
- Adda newGroup Productand set up pricing, availability and fencing with theDefinition,Defaults, andSeasonstabs.
- If needed, use the arrowsto change the display order of your products.

## Best Practices

### Understand Group Product Configurations

You configure the minimum and maximum rooms to define your room ranges. The room ranges must not overlap between each product and must be sequential with no gaps. For example, group product A has a minimum room count of 5 and maximum of 18, therefore, group product B must start with a minimum room count of 19.
