# Days to Arrival

Use Days to Arrival to vary the discount of your linked products based on days to arrival. For example, you might want to offer a larger discount for guests who book  30-60 days to arrival than for those that book 0-30 days to arrival. In this case, create ranges of Day of Arrival-30, 31-60, and, if needed, 61+.
Once set up, you can apply the arrival ranges to products in theDefaultsandSeasonstabs. Note that to support this,most selling systemsrequire multiple rate codes, each with differing days to arrival fencing or restrictions.

## Setup Steps

### Setting up Days to Arrival

- Click, thenDecisions, and thenPricing.
- Click Advanced Settings.
- ClickDays to Arrival.
- Set up the Days to Arrival ranges:
- ClickSave.

### Adding New Ranges to Existing Products

If you add a new range and already have products with Days to Arrival pricing in the Defaults or Seasons tab, the products become inactive. A warning icondisplays for them on thePricing Configurationpage, because their setup is incomplete. Edit the products to set up their pricing adjustments for the new Days to Arrival range. Then select the Active checkbox on the Available Products page to reactivate the product. If the product's Upload checkbox was selected, you need to reselect it.
