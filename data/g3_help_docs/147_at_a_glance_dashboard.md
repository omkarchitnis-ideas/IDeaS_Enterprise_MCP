# At a Glance Dashboard

Use the At a Glance dashboard to decide what periods you need to focus on. For example, what low-demand periods need help, are there any exceptions to the usual demand patterns, or how is this month doing compared to last yearor budget.

### Which Panel Do You Want to View?

- TheCalendarlets you quickly see the low and high demand days for the current and next month.
- In theSummary, quickly compare this month's performance to other periods, like same month last year.
- In theMonthly Booking Pace, review how your occupancy builds and compare that to last year.
- View the dailyRevPARand how that compares with the same day of the week last year.
- View howOccupancy and ADRimpacted RevPAR for a deeper analysis.
- InOccupancy and Revenue, view what type of business makes up the daily on books and forecast.
