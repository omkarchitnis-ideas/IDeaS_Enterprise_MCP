G3 RMSchecks the conditions of most Alerts duringnightly processingAlso known as Business Day End or BDE. The standard daily system update that runs during overnight hours after the end of the business day..For theUnassigned Market Segmentand Unassigned Room Type Alerts, it also checks duringintraday processingAbbreviated IDP, it's a system update that occurs between nightly updates. ## Steps to Resolve

The details about an  Alert provide instructions and links that direct you to other areas 
 ofG3 RMS, where you can investigate and correct the condition that caused the Alert. After you fix the issue,G3 RMSupdates the Alert score after the next nightly optimization. If it finds that the issue is resolved, the score is set to zero, and the Alert State changes to Resolved. If the issue 
 still remains, the Alert's score increases.
