# Reputation Competitor Mapping

WithReputation ManagementG3 RMScombines the publicly available data from rate shopping and  reputation management providers to improve pricing (ifmarket data is enabledBy default, the RMS can use market data (Rate Shopping, Demand360, or Reputation) to improve its forecasts and controls (like pricing or LRV). If needed, the system can ignore such data in its optimization. For details, open the Important Information topic and review the Market Data section.). Use the Competitor Mapping - Reputation tab to match the competitors from these two data sets, which come from different vendors (who often use different competitor names).
You must match at least three rate shopping and reputation competitors that are checked forUse Rate Shopping Data. One match can be your property. The more matching competitors, the better decisionsG3 RMSproduces. If you have no matching competitors, ask your reputation management vendor to add competitors.
