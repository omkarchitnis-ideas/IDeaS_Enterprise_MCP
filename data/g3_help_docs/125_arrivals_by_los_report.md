# Arrivals by LOS Report

Use the Arrivals By LOS report to understand your property's length of stay distribution. Analyze the number of arrivals by length of stay for a period to find patterns and exceptions, for example, dates with lower-than-usual share of longer stays when you might change pricing.
And if your share of LOS 6+ business is consistently high, consider usingExtended Stay Forecasting, contact your IDeaS representative for details.
