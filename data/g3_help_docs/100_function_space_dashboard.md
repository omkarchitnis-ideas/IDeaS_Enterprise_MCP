# Function Space Dashboard

Make better Function Space decisions by using the four panels of the Dashboard to understand your business patterns and trends. Start with an overview, for example, revenues by month and day of week, then drill down further, for example, to compare by market segment.
- Function Space Utilization and Efficiency
- Utilization Details
- Revenue by Market Segment, Function Room, Revenue Stream
- Revenue Contribution by Market Segment, Day of Week and Revenue Stream

## Dashboard Steps

### Access the Dashboard

Clickand thenDashboard.

### Interact with the Dashboard

- Select the year that you want to view. For revenue contribution, you also select the month.
- Select to view by Day Part or Day of Week in the Function Space Utilization and Efficiency  panel.
- Click the export iconto export the details of a panel to a spreadsheet.
- Point to elements in the charts, like bars or lines in a graph, to see numerical values.
- Click an item in the legend to hide the item from the chart. Click it again to restore it.

## Data Details

All data uses only:
- Indivisible Function Rooms that are set toIncludeYesin theFunction Roomtab. Note that  Function Rooms set toYescan include revenue from  Function Rooms set toNo, seeDisplay Revenue As.
- Day Parts that are not checked asExcludein theDay Partstab of Configuration.
- Event Typesthat are not checked as Out of Service.
Up to five years of data are available, the current and the two past and the two future years.  The current year includes actual (past dates) and on books data (future dates). SeeFunction Space Calculationsfor details on the utilization and efficiency metrics.

### Function Space Utilization and Efficiency

The three charts display totals for the selected Analysis and Comparison years, broken out by Day of Week or Day Part. You can view a specific month or the full year.

#### Function Space Utilization

Shows the percentage of how much the available Function Space is used.

#### Revenue Producing Utilization Only

Same as Function Space Utilization, but excludes complimentary and house-use bookings that didn't generate revenues.

#### Function Space Efficiency

Shows the percentage of Function Space Utilization that generates revenues. In other words, the Revenue Producing Utilization divided by the total Function Space Utilization.

### Utilization Details

#### Utilization by Week

Displays Function Space Utilization percentage by month and week for the selected year. Use the heat map to quickly detect patterns. Weeks are by days of a month, not day of week. For example, Week 1 includes the first seven days of a month. Week 5 includes the remaining days for the month and is empty if the month contains only 28 days.

#### Utilization by Room and Day Parts

Displays Function Space Utilization percentage for each of your indivisible Function Rooms and for the selected year. Point to the segment of a Function Room to see the utilization by Day Part. The Utilization percentages of all Function Rooms add up to the total utilization value in the center.

### Revenue by Market Segment, Function Room, Revenue Stream

Displays the totals of theConference & Banquetrevenues by month and day of week. A heat map and a line graph help you understand how much each day of week contributed to the total revenue of the month.
Use the filters to  display revenues for specific combinations ofMarket Segments, Function Rooms, and Conference & Banquet revenue streams.

### Revenue Contribution by Market Segment, Day of Week and Revenue Stream

For a selected month and year, view the Conference & Banquet revenue contribution by market segment, day of week, and by revenue stream. Point to a segment to view the revenues by Day Part.
These graphs help you understand the monthly revenue totals of the previous table. For example, for last April's revenues, what was the distribution by market segment, day of week, and by revenue stream?
