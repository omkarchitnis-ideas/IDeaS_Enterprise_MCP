# Override Demand by Arrival by LOS

Arrival by LOS overrides allow you to share information withG3 RMSabout demand for a specific length of stay (LOS). For example, you know that an upcoming promotion impacts the demand only for stays longer than three nights. Before overriding the demand, review theBest Practices.
Arrival by LOS overrides are not available:
- On dates with existingOccupancy Dateoverrides.
On dates with existingOccupancy Dateoverrides.
