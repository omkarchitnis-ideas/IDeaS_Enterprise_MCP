# Important Information

Use the Important Information window to view key system statuses and settings, like what data the system uses and when data was last updated.

## Steps to Open Important Information

In the top right corner of anyG3 RMSpage, click Important Information.

## Data Details

You only see this section ifG3 RMSdoesnotuse  market data (Rate Shopping, Demand360, or Reputation) to improve its forecasts and controls (like pricing or LRV). If you see this section, it means that you can view market data like competitor pricing inG3 RMSbut that the system ignores all such external data in its optimization. Instead,G3 RMSrelies on your property's own internal data from the reservations system to create the best possible   forecasts and controls.
