# Benefit Measurement

Benefit Measurement allows you to see the return on yourG3 RMSinvestment. You can view the estimated direct gains from usingG3 RMScompared to the previous revenue management approach.
G3 RMSautomatically updates the benefits for the past three months by the 20th of every month. For key metrics, like occupancy and ADR, you can view and export the actual performance withG3 RMSversus the estimated previous approach.  If you are responsible for multiple properties, you can view the benefits byProperty Group.
SeeSimulatedin the Data Details for how the system determines the benefits. For comparison of other benefit measurement approaches, seeChallenges for Measuring Benefits.
Note: Benefit measurement is unavailable for months when:
- More than 70% of your rooms was out of order.
- Decision DeliveryA two-way status when the RMS receives data from the reservation system, produces forecasts and ouputs (like pricing), and sends outputs to the selling system.began less than 2 months  prior.

## Steps to Run Benefit Measurement

- Clickand thenBenefit Measurement.
- If needed, change the selected months. If available, you can view the benefits for up to 12 of the past 24 months.
- ClickDisplay.The benefits display for the last three months.
- Clickto open the details for a specific month.
- Clickto download  the results to an Excel spreadsheet.
- If you manage multiple properties, you can view the benefits byProperty Group. You can searchfor a property and clickto see the details for the property.
