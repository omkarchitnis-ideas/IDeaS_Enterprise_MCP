# RevPAR Chart

Use the RevPAR chart to see RevPAR by occupancy day and how the RevPAR compares 
 with the same day of the week last year (not the same calendar date). Click to see anoverview of all panels of the dashboard.

## Chart Steps

### Accessing the RevPAR Chart

- Clickand thenAt a Glance.
- Scroll down the page to the RevPAR chart.

- Hide either this year or last yearâs data by clicking on their labels in the legend.
- Point to a date to see the exact values in a window.
- Use the zoom feature to focus on a subset of the data.

## Data Details

For past dates, the RevPAR chart shows actual RevPAR for both this year and last year. For current or future dates, the chart shows the forecasted RevPAR for this year compared to the actual RevPAR for last year.
By default, this value uses the Physical Capacity for the total number of rooms, which includes out of order rooms.A property (or a corporate office for an enterprise) can contact IDeaS to switch this to use Effective Capacity, which excludes out of order rooms.
To drill down into more details about Occupancy and ADR, use the neighboringOccupancy and ADRchart.
