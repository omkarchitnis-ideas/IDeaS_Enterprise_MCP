# Reputation Performance

Use the Reputation Performance chart to understand how your reputation score impacts your performance. For the selected past period, the chart compares your property's reputation score againstRevPARRevenue Per Available Room. The total room revenue divided by the total number of rooms (capacity).
See the Property Information topic for the capacity definition., by month and for this and the previous year. This helps you evaluate changes and trends in performance over time, seescenarios.

### Interacting with Reputation Performance

- The default period for the chart is 12 historical months. If data is available for less than 12 months, only the months for which the data is available display.
- The X-axis shows the selected time frame by month.
- The primary Y-axis measures historical reputation scores.
- The secondary Y-axis measures your selected Key Performance Indicator.
- Bars represent the average reputation score by month.
- Click an item in the legend to remove it from the display.
- Point to data points or the bars to view the reputation and performance values.
- Click the export iconto export the data behind the chart to a spreadsheet.
- ClickResetto reset the period and Key Performance Indicator filters back to the default values.

## Scenarios

Use the Reputation Performance chart  to do the following:

### Identify Opportunities to Drive Rates

For example, an increase in your average reputation score should be accompanied by an increase in average ADR. If that's not the case,  you might have an opportunity to increase rates.

### Quantify Revenue Opportunities

As you accumulate reputation data, use the chart to quantify the revenue opportunities that you gained by integrating your reputation performance into your revenue management strategies.
