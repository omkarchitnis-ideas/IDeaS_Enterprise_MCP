# Override History

Use the Override History tab to review if a pricing override existed for a selected date and Room Class. This is useful when you investigate  a date, since past pricing overrides might have impacted the performance of that date. SeeBest Practices for Overriding Pricingfor more information.

## Steps to Review

- Clickand thenPricing.
- Adjust the selections so the table displays the dates, Room Class and Room Type for 
	 which you want to view the data.
- Click the value of theFinal Pricefor the date and room type. The Override window opens.
- Click theOverride Historytab.

### Actions

- At the top, review the selected arrival date and Room Class.My Priceis theFinal PriceThe value of the pricing output that the RMS sends to the selling systems. Final Price is based on the Optimal Price, after applying rounding rules, offsets and supplements (if applicable). Final Price also includes your configured tax value, if you are using tax-inclusive (VAT) pricing.for theBase Room TypeThe one room type in each Room Class on which the RMS bases its pricing for the other room types in the Room Class., or the final pricing decision thatG3 RMSsends to yourselling system. The arrow next toMy Priceindicates an increase or decrease since the last processing.
- Click the back arrowor forward arrowto move to the previous or next date. The available dates are limited to your date selection on the Pricing page.
- Change the selectedRoom Class. The options are limited by your Room Class selection on the Pricing page.
- Click the notes iconto view and add notes for the date. A check mark on the notes iconindicates that notes already exist.

## Data Details

The Override History tab shows the override history information for all dates. The most recent change displays at the top of the list.
