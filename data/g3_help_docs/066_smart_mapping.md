# Smart Mapping

If your property subscribes toRate Data Advantage, use Smart Mapping to ensure thatG3 RMScompares prices for similar room types and products when it uses publicly available competitor prices to optimize results. For example, comparing your competitors' price for Standard room types and the Best Flexible product  to your  Standard/Best Flexible price, not your   Suite/Advance Purchase price.

## Best Practices

### Understand Your Role in Mapping Rate Shopping Results

Whenever possibleG3 RMScompletes the mapping for you and displays this iconnext to the result. You can map any cards in  the Unmapped section or wait until the next system mapping. Cards are unmapped because they are  new, for example, due to  a new room type or a   new channel, or when you use Synthetic  Data. Whether you map yourself or review the system's mapping, ensure that:
- Each Competitor Room Class (how the rate shopping provider groups room types) is mapped to your property'sG3 RMSRoom Class of comparable value.
Each Competitor Room Class (how the rate shopping provider groups room types) is mapped to your property'sG3 RMSRoom Class of comparable value.
- Each card representing one rate shopping result is mapped to the correct Room Class pair.You don't have to map Uncategorized cards.G3 RMSreceives a large amount of competitor data, so it's often better to skip cards without a good match. If the card's ADR changes and becomes a closer match,G3 RMSwill suggest a mapping that during its  regular checks.
