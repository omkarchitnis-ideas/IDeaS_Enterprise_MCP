# Rate Data Advantage

Rate Data Advantageis a subscription thatenablesG3 RMSto optimally use the publicly available pricing of your competitors for improving its forecasts and outputs like pricing. See thebenefitscompared to Standard Rate Shopping.

## Benefits of Rate Data Advantage

With a Rate Data Advantage subscription your property'sG3 RMSbenefits from the most detailed rate shopping data. Standard Rate Shopping providesG3 RMSonly with  competitor prices for   a single rate type (usually the Best Flexible rate code, often calledthe primary priced product). But Rate Data Advantage supplies  more granular competitor information and helps the system understand pricing by:
- Rate types, for example,  Best Flexible, Advance Purchase, or Semi-Flexible - Loyalty. This helps the system better optimize your pricing products like Advance Purchase,  seeProduct Definitionfor  details.
