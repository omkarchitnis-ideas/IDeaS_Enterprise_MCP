## Data Details

- The X-axis measures the reputation score for your property and your competitors.
- The Y-axis measures the price for your property and your competitors.
- The horizontal line represents the average price for your property and your competitors.
- The vertical line represents the average reputation score for your property and your competitors.
- Your property and your competitors' properties are plotted according to where the lines intersect, using both rate shopping and reputation management data. The quadrants represent these four positions:
- For past dates, the chart uses the average reputation scores and the competitor prices shopped for each date.
- For future dates, the chart displays the average of competitor rates shopped for each date and the last recorded reputation scores.

## Best Practices

### Remove Unnecessary Competitors

The chart includes all your mapped competitors for whichG3 RMSreceives both rate shopping and reputation data. The set includes those that you chose to ignore inIgnore Competitor Datafor the selected period. If you want to remove these competitors from the display, click their name in the legend.

### Review Periods Longer Than Two Weeks

Avoid reviewing the chart for date ranges of less than two weeks. Shorter date ranges are heavily influenced by outliers, which could lead to invalid conclusions of your market position over time.

### EnsureG3 RMSReceives Competitor Data

If there is no rate shopping or reputation data available for a competitor for a date or date range, the competitor does not display in the chart. A rate shopping competitor also does not display if it does not have a reputation management competitor mapped against it inCompetitor Mapping â Reputation.

## Scenarios

Use the Reputation Quadrant to do the following:

### Verify Your Pricing

Verify if your prices align with your guests' perception of your hotel's value. For example:
- If your property falls into the top left quadrant, your online reviews and ratings are not aligned with your rates.
- If your property falls into the bottom right quadrant, you might have an opportunity to align your rates with your higher reputation score.

### Validate Your Competitive Set

Competitors that consistently display as outliers may not be appropriate competitors and may need to be excluded from use in the system.
