# Overbookingor Cost of WalkOverrides

Use the  Override Details window in Overbooking Management to monitor and, if necessary, override overbookingor the cost of walk. On the Room Type tab, you can override at the property or room type level and one day at a time. You can review relevant data, like capacity and on books.
