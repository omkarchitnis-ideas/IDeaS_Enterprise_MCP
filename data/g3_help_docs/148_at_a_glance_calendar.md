# At a Glance Calendar

Use the calendar to view  expected occupancy, Average Daily Rate (ADRAverage Daily Rate. Room revenue divided by the number of rooms of occupancy.), and Revenue per Available Room (RevPARRevenue Per Available Room. The total room revenue divided by the total number of rooms (capacity).
See the Property Information topic for the capacity definition.) for a two-month period. Color-coding helps you quickly view an overview of your high and low demand periods and to understand which days require your attention. It also helps you detect day-of-week patterns and exceptions to those patterns.
View additional details in a window by clicking a calendar date. Click to see anoverview of all panels of the dashboard.

## Calendar Steps

- Clickand thenAt a Glance.The At a Glance calendar displays at the top of the page. The legend at the base of the 
	 calendars indicates the colors associated with the occupancy percentage for each date in the calendar.You can customize the colors and occupancy range, seeHeat Map.
- Click the date button  to select the starting month orfiscal periodthat you want to monitor. The system displays data for two months or fiscal periods. Click the right arrowor left arrowto select a different year.
- Click the open 
	 iconin the upper right corner to display the table in full screen view. Close the window to return to the full dashboard.
- Click a date to display more data in a separate window.
- To view additional data on the other side of the window, click the flip iconin the lower right corner.
- At the bottom of the window clickLast Yearto view  last year's data. Last Year's data shows the comparable values achieved for the same day of the week last year*. Occupancy and revenue values show the difference 
 between current on-books and last year as  the Variance, expressed 
 in percentage points.
- ClickDecisionsto view the decisions for 
	 LRV, Overbooking, and BAR (or your name for theprimary priced productMain pricing decision that the RMS optimizes. Usually this is the lowest non-restricted product with flexible cancellation policy that anyone can book. The default name is Best Available Rate (BAR), but you can change it in Pricing Configuration.)  at the property level. The following data 
	 elements (in alphabetical order) display in the window and its flip side for the selected date. Data elements are for the current or future dates unless noted.
- ClickCloseto close the 
	 windows, or click the flip iconagain to close the two side windows 
	 and return to the original window.
