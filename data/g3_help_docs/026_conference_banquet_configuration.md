# Conference & Banquet  Configuration

Add Conference & Banquet revenue streams and their profit percentage to improve Group Evaluations.
If you are using Function Space rather than Group Pricing, seeFunction Space Conference & Banquet setup.

### What Are Conference & Banquet Revenue Streams?

Conference and Banquet revenue streams are additional sources of income from groups such as meeting room rental, audio-visual equipment rental or banquet food and beverage. They help you understand a group's overall value aside from their room revenue contribution.
For example, a group of 20 rooms at $100 displaces 20 Transient rooms at $120, which seems to tell you to not accept that group. However, if the group also has $5,000 Conference & Banquet revenue, with a profit of $2,900, then you might accept the group. The evaluation results display this data to help you make a decision about potential group business.
Conference & Banquet revenue doesn't impact the calculation of the Break Even and Recommended Rates becauseG3 RMSdoesn't forecast Conference & Banquet displacement.

## Setup Steps

### Adding Revenue Streams

- Click, thenDecisions, and thenGroup Pricing Configuration.
- Click theConference & Banquettab.
- Click to adda new revenue stream.
- Enter a unique name for theConference and Banquet Revenue Stream.
- Enter theProfit %value for the stream. This value is the average percentage of profit  that your property generates from the revenue stream. Its calculation varies, but, if needed, a finance department usually can provide  this data.
- Click to savethe changes.

### Deleting a Revenue Stream

G3 RMSremoves deleted Conference & Banquet revenue streams from existing saved evaluations only when the group is re-evaluated. For  properties integrated with  Delphi, you can only delete revenue streams that are configured inG3 RMS. Revenue streams that are configured in Delphi must be deleted there.
- Click, thenDecisions, and thenGroup Pricing Configuration.
- Click theConference & Banquettab.
- Click to deletetheConference and Banquet Revenue Stream. A confirmation window opens.
- ClickOK.
