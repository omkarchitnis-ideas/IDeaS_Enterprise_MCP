# Occupancy and Revenue

The Occupancy and Revenue chart shows you how much each type of business contributes to overall occupancy, how the business mix relates to total revenue, and how on-books values compare to their forecast.
If you are using afiscal calendar, data displays for fiscal periods rather than months. Click to see anoverview of all panels of the dashboard.

## Chart Steps

### Accessing the Chart

- Clickand thenAt a Glance.
- Scroll down the page to the Occupancy and Revenue chart.

### Interacting with the Chart

- Click the filter iconto select On Books or Forecast data for a specificProperty Business VieworCorporate Business View.
- Click a label in the legend to 
	 view or hide the associated bar or line graph.
- Point to a data point to view specific data:Bar charts show on-books or forecasted occupancy %.Line graphs show on-books or forecasted revenue.
- Bar charts show on-books or forecasted occupancy %.
- Line graphs show on-books or forecasted revenue.
- Click and drag over the chart to zoom in on a specific period.
- ClickReset zoomto return to the full month view.

## Data Details

The bars represent occupancy values, with the value axis on the left side. The top of the chart shows 100% capacity. The left bar shows the occupancy on-books, and the right bar shows the forecasted occupancy. The variance is the forecasted pick-up.
Each occupancy bar is broken down byProperty Business Viewsfor single properties orCorporate Business Viewsfor multiple properties.
The total room revenue on the books and total revenue forecasted are represented by line graphs, with the value axis on the right.
