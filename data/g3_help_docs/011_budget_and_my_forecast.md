# Budget and My Forecast

Use the optional Budget and My Forecast to compareG3 RMSmetrics against your own budget and forecast data, for example, to see if the occupancy forecast is above or below the budget.
After you enable the modules on the Configuration tab (seesteps), you can enter the Budget and My Forecast data. Then you can view that data in theAt a Glance SummaryandBusiness Analysis Data Detailsdashboards and in theData Extractionreport.
Note: Budget and My Forecast are for reporting purposes and don't impact the system's forecasts and decisions.
