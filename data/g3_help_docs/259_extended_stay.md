### Extended Stay Forecasting and Optimization

By default, you view and override demand for lengths of stay (LOS) of one to eight nights. If a lot of business at your property stays longer than eight nights, the Extended Stay Forecasting and Optimization feature helps you manage that demand.
Once enabled,G3 RMSanalyzes the amount and distribution of booking data beyond LOS8 and  might create groupings, like LOS 9-20, 21-36 etc. Otherwise, it displays LOS9+. You can thenviewandoverride demandfor LOS greater than eight. The feature also impacts how you set upmarket segmentsand requires the use ofstayed (vs. booked)data.
If you offer specific rate codes for Extended Stay, we recommend that you useLinkedorIndependentproducts to price them.
