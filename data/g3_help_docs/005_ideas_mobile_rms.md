# IDeaS Mobile RMS

The mobile app forG3 RMSis free and available for both Android and iOS phones and tablets. In the mobile app you can:
- View monthly, weekly and daily snapshots of performance metrics
- Review key performance metrics
- Review, override and sync pricing decisions
- Review operational forecasts
Note: The mobile app is not available in mainland China and is not supported  for iOS 12.0 or lower.

## Functions in the App

### Pricing Overrides

Open the Pricing page from the main menu or by tappingBAR Overridefrom the daily Business Details view. For the selected date, the Pricing page displays the Last Room Value, Competitor Rate (if available) and Final Price. If Competitor data is available, tap the value to view the publicly available prices of your competitors by Channel.
Move to the previous or next date by tapping the arrows next to the date  or by tapping the calendar icon.

### Removing an Override

Open the Pricing page or tap a date in the At a Glance calendar to open the daily Business Details view. Tap the BAR Value link with the override icon for the pricing override you wish to revert. TapRemove Overrideon the override screen and tap the check mark in the upper right-hand corner to remove the override.

### Uploading Decisions

Tap toUpload Decisionin the At A Glance calendar. Note that you can't upload the pricing decisions duringRead Only(when you see the lock icon) and if you don't have the Upload permission inG3 RMS.

## Data in the App

Data displays at the individual property level. Tap the property selector icon   to select a different property. In User Preferences, you can define the Default Property to display when you sign in.

### At a Glance Calendar

For each future day in the selected month, the calendar displays BAR, Occupancy Forecast, On-Books, Occupancy color codes, Floor and User Overrides, Special Events, and theSystem DateDisplaying in the top right of each G3 RMS page, it's the time when  the RMS last received data from your reservation system. If the data is late, you get an Alert..
Historical dates display BAR and the actual occupancy achieved.

### Monthly Summary

The Monthly Summary below the calendar displays business details for the month that include the Occupancy, Room Revenue, ADR, and RevPAR that are On Books,Expected, and Budgeted.
Historical months display Actualsand Budgetdata for Occupancy, Room Revenue, ADR, and RevPAR.

### Daily Business Details

Tap a date in the At a Glance calendar to view daily business details, which include BAR, Occupancy Forecast, Occupancy On Books, Occupancy Last Year, On Books Last Year, Rooms Left to Sell, Out of Order, LRV, Room Revenue, and Overbooking.
The business details for historical dates displays Actual Occupancy, Occupancy Last Year, On Books Last Year, Out of Order, and LRV.

### Weekly Business Details and Graphs

Tap a week number on the left side of the At a Glance calendar to access business details for that week.
This view displays Rooms Sold, ADR, and RevPAR graphs for the week. These show On Books and Demand data, as well as comparing On Books data to last year. The Business Details for current and future dates displays Occupancy Forecast, Occupancy On Books, Occupancy Last Year (LY), On Books Last Year (LY), Rooms Left to Sell, Out of Order, LRV, BAR, and Room Revenue for each day of the week.
For historical dates, the graphs display achieved values. The weekly Business Details for historical dates displays Actuals for Occupancy, Occupancy Last Year, On Books LY, Out of Order, LRV, and BAR.

### Operations Report

Open the Operations Report from the main menu . The Operations Report shows Capacity, Out of Order (OOO), Occupancy, Arrivals, Departures, Stay-Thrus, and Special Events for a 21-day operational forecast window. The app allows you to toggle between Forecast and On Books views. SeeOperations Reportfor more information.
