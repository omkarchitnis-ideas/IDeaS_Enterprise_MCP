## Setup Steps

- Click, thenForecasts, and thenSynthetic Data.
- Click theMarket Segmenttab.
- Click the add iconto open a row for a new market segment.
- Enter theCodefor the market segment. Each market segment code must be unique.
- Enter theNamefor the market segment.
- Enter an optionalDescription.
- Click the save icon. A confirmation window opens. ClickYesif you are certain that the information is correct.
For the next steps, review the Synthetic DataWork Flow.
