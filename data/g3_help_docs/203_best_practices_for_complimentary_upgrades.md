# Best Practices for Complimentary Upgrades

## Book the Reservation in the Booked Room Type, Then Move It

If a guest books a Standard room type and receives a complimentary upgrade to a Suite, you should book the reservation in the Standard room and save it. Only then move the reservation to the Suite, even if you offered the upgrade automatically at the point of reservation. This way you ensure that thereservation systemthat provides data tothe RMS(typically your PMS or CRS)records the demand against the booked room type and thatthe RMSreceives the information about both the stayedandthe booked room type.

## Balance Your Inventory at the Right Time

If you don't override overbooking, balance your room types as close to the day of arrival as possible. This way, additional demand for higher Room Classes can book even if the forecasted demand by Room Class might be off.
There are two scenarios when you want to balance your room types early:
- If you overbooked your property beyond the system's overbooking decision. This can happen when you accept a group or override overbooking. Earlier balancing of your room types avoids further overbooking.
- If your channel manager or distribution system sees only room type, but not house level availability. Without balancing and when there are rooms left to sell in some room types, overbooking can increase.
