### Price Sensitivity

Measures how pricing impacts demand, and vice versa.G3 RMScalculates price sensitivity for each day and for all unqualified and linked qualified demand, by Forecast Group, Room Class, season, day of week and time left to arrival.
Demand is either elastic or inelastic. For example, a budget leisure traveler who pays for their own stay is likely very price elastic. And a business traveler whose company pays for a luxury hotel stay is likely less responsive to changes in pricing (either up or down) and price inelastic.
