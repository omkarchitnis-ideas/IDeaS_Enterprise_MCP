### Data Extracts

Also called Snapshots. They are files with new and changed booking data thatG3 RMSreceives from thereservation systemThe primary reservation system, like a PMS or CRS, that provides data to the RMS%]. The data from that one system is used by the RMS to forecast, optimize and produce controls. The controls are sent to all selling systems, which for some integrations may exclude the reservation system.during Processing. They include transactional and summary data.
- Summary data is top level data, similar to what is produced by areservation systemafter the night audit, like the sum of all rooms that were sold for a specific market segment.
- Transactional data are the details from individual reservations, like their rate code, market segment, or status. You can view transactional data in theBusiness Insightsdashboard. To view whenG3 RMSlast loaded transactional data, click Important Informationin the top right of any page.
G3 RMSuses and displays both types, based on what's available and most appropriate. For example, on the Business Analysis dashboard, Data Details, if you look  at Market Segment level or higher, the data is based on summary data. At the  Rate Code level the data is added up from the transactional reservation data.
This data exchange is similar to how yourreservation systemshares transaction and summary data with theselling systemthat your brand website uses, like available capacity (summary) and   reservation details (transactional). If you  notice display differences between summary and transactional data, seedata differencesfor details.
