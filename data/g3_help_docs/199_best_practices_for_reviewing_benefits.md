# Best Practices for Reviewing Benefits

## Consider the Challenges of any Method to Measure the Benefits

You might ask if the benefits measured bythe RMSare reliable. But no approach can exactly measure how your property would have performed without the system.
- The RMSanalyzes historical reservations and results to create a model of the previous revenue management approach and applies that model to the observed period. It uses the same bookings, inventory, market conditions, and demand. It is an imperfect but consistent statistical approach that many of our clients trust and use.
- Alternative approaches have other downsides that make them ineffective:Year-on-Year Comparisons: A year-on-year comparison does not consider changes, like market conditions, promotional programs, pricing, competitor changes, economic conditions, and changing revenue management practices. Therefore such a  comparison is inaccurate.Side-by-Side Property Comparisons: If you are managing multiple properties, you might run half your properties with and the other half withoutthe RMS. But that would still include differences between the properties outside the control ofthe RMS, like market conditions and competitors.
