# Multiday Demand Override by Arrival by LOS

Use Multiday Overrides by Arrival by LOS to share information withG3 RMSabout demand that impacts only a specific length of stay (LOS) and a longer period. For example, you know that an upcoming promotion impacts the demand for arrivals in July and August and only for stays longer than three nights.
Note: Due to their large impact, use multiday overrides with caution. Review thebest practicesbefore you override.
