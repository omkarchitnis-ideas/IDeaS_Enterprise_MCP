# Channel Forecast Dashboard

Use the Channel Forecast dashboard to understand channel costs and tobetter manage channels. After you import thechannel costs, use the dashboard to view the expected amount of business and costs by channel and by month.G3 RMSforecasts a channel's business based on its past share of the total business.
The dashboard consists of six panels at the top and three graphs below:
- Net ADR Forecast
- Net RevPAR Forecast
- Channel Cost Forecast
- Net Revenue Forecast
- Occupancy Forecast
- Average Cost Forecast
- Net Channel Revenue
- Channel Occupancy
Before you use the dashboard, ensure that your subscription includes it, thatG3 RMSusesContinuous Pricing, is not on Synthetic Data and not using aFiscal Calendar.

## Steps

### Access the Dashboard

Clickand thenChannel Forecast.

### Interact with the Dashboard

- Select theChannelsorSourcesthat you want to view. For all the following selections you can adefault setting:Channel: select up to five channels.Source: select up to five sources and view the totals for each selected source across all channels. This is useful for sources that can be booked with multiple channels.Source by Channel: select up to five sources per channel and view the totals for each source.
- Channel: select up to five channels.
- Source: select up to five sources and view the totals for each selected source across all channels. This is useful for sources that can be booked with multiple channels.
- Source by Channel: select up to five sources per channel and view the totals for each source.
- Select theMonthsthat you want to view. By default, the current and the next five months display. You can select only future months.
- Select to view all days of the week, or onlyWeekdayorWeekend. You can define weekend and weekday inForecast Settings.
- Select to view the data in a chartor a table.
- Turn on theDailytoggle to view daily instead of monthly channel costs.
- Hover over elements in the charts, like bars or lines in a graph, to see numerical values.
- Click an item in the legend to hide the item from the chart. Click it again to show it.

### Default View Settings

- Select your preferredView Type, Chart or Table.

- Based on your  selection in the preceding step, define  the default channels.

## Data in Channel Forecast Dashboard

Note that your selection of Channels or Sources applies only to the two bottom graphs, not to the top six panels.

### Net ADR Forecast

This panel displays the forecasted net ADR (excluding channel costs) for the selected months and days. Compare that to the forecasted gross ADR (including channel costs) at property level and for the same months and days. You can also view the percentage difference between the two values.

### Net RevPAR Forecast

This panel displays the forecasted netRevPARRevenue Per Available Room. The total room revenue divided by the total number of rooms (capacity).
See the Property Information topic for the capacity definition.(excluding channel costs) of the selected months and days compared to the forecasted gross RevPAR (including channel costs) at property level and for the same months and days. You can also view the percentage difference between the two values.

### Channel Cost Forecast

For the selected months and days, compare the channel costs of the forecasted business to last year's actual channel costs. You can also view the percentage change between the two values.G3 RMSuses the forecasted costs to calculate Net ADR and Net RevPAR.

### Net Revenue Forecast

The panel  displays the forecasted net revenues (excluding channel costs) of the selected months and days compared to the forecasted gross revenues (including channel costs) at property level and for the same months and days. You can also view the percentage difference between the two values.
Click the panel to view the details of the net revenues in a graph below. The bars represent the total net revenue for each selected month. The lines represent the total net revenue for each selected channel and month. Both display data for the selected days of the week.

### Occupancy Forecast

The panel displays the Occupancy Forecast for all channels for the selected months and days. You can compare that to last year's actual Occupancy percentage for the same months and days. You can also view the percentage change between the two values.
Click the panel to view a graph that shows the occupancy for each of the selected channels  compared to the occupancy for all selected channels and how that changes by month. The bars represent the total (all selected channels) forecasted occupancy percentage for each selected month. The lines represent the forecasted occupancy percentage for each selected channel and month. Both display data for the selected days of week.

### Average Cost Forecast

The panel displays the forecasted average channel cost per room, based on all channels and the selected months and days. It is calculated by dividing the total Channel Cost Forecast by the number of forecasted rooms. You can compare the forecast to last year's average channel cost per sold room. You can also view the percentage change.
Click the panel to view a graph with the details. The bars represent the average forecasted channel cost for all channels and for each selected month. The lines represent the average forecasted channel cost for each selected channel and month. Both display data for the selected days of week.

### Net Channel Revenue

For each selected month or day, this bar chart displays the forecasted net revenues  (excluding channel costs) next to the net on books revenues, separated by weekdays and weekends. Color coding shows how much each channel contributes to the monthly total.

### Channel Occupancy

For each selected month or day, this bar chart displays the forecasted occupancy in number of rooms next to the on books occupancy, separated by weekdays and weekends. Color coding shows how much each channel contributes to the monthly total.

## Best Practices

### Use the Dashboard to Understand Channel Costs

The dashboard helps you learn how channel costs impact your key metrics, like RevPAR. This knowledge helps you make better decisions about which channels your property should work with and at what costs. Or use that understanding to improve your strategies about which promotions and offers are available on which channels.
