## Data Details

Use the chart data to review the system's decision and theDemand and Washforecast that are the basis for those decisions.
- Capacity in the graph refers toPhysical CapacityThe total number of guest rooms at a property, including out of order rooms..
- Effective Capacity is the Physical Capacity minus out of order rooms.
- The left axis corresponds to the number of rooms. Use the bar charts to review On Books, Remaining Demand, and Occupancy Forecast.
- The right axis represents pricing values. Use the line graphs that represent theCeiling, Floor, and theBARBest Available Rate. The lowest non-restricted product with flexible cancellation policy that anyone can book. The RMS optimizes the pricing of the BAR product. Other products, like Advanced Purchase or packages, can be linked to the BAR price.decision to see the relationship between the pricing decision and the available pricing.
